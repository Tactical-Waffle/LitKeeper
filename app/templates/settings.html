Downloads

After each story it fetches from Literotica, LitKeeper waits before starting the next. The wait changes every time, up to 25% shorter or longer, so downloads don't arrive like clockwork. 0 means no wait.

minutes, about

A download that fails (for example when Cloudflare asks for a check) is tried again after about 5, 15 and 45 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours, before it is marked failed. Failures in a row also lengthen the wait before the next story.
